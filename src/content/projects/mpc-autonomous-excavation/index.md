Developed a Latency-Aware Model Predictive Control (MPC) strategy for autonomous
excavator arms. Additionally, I integrated a collision-aware trajectory planner
to ensure safety in dynamic environments. The solution was tested in both
simulation and on physical excavators.

Applied work carried out with [Gravis Robotics](https://www.gravisrobotics.com).
